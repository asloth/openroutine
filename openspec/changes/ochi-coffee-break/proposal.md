## Why

A long step, like 20 minutes of reading or a slow morning tidy, leaves Ochi sitting still for the
whole stretch. Sara asked for Ochi to have a cup of coffee while a step runs long, so the pet feels
like it's settling in to keep you company.

## What Changes

- Add a coffee mug, a paw on its handle, and two steam wisps to the Rive mascot, plus three states:
  `coffee_in` brings the mug up, `coffee_hold` holds it while the steam drifts, and `coffee_sip`
  lifts it to the mouth for a slow sip with the happy squint.
- Add a `hasCoffee` boolean and a `sip` trigger to the `Mascot` view model.
- `MascotSlot` gains `hasCoffee`, and `MascotReaction` gains `sip`.
- On the running timer, a step planned for more than 5 minutes gets the coffee from the moment it
  starts, and Ochi sips about every 45 seconds. Pausing puts the cup down so Ochi can doze. Done,
  Skip, and Do it last tuck the cup away for the reaction, and it comes back after if the next step
  is long too.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `timer-run` — the running mascot holds a coffee through long steps.

## Impact

**Code.** `app/lib/widgets/mascot_slot.dart`, `app/lib/screens/timer/timer_screen.dart`, and
`app/assets/mascot.riv`. The Rive source is `rive/mascot/scene.rml`, generated by
`rive/mascot/build/gen_coffee.py`. No new packages.

**Tests.** Six widget tests in `app/test/screens/timer/timer_screen_test.dart` and two unit tests in
`app/test/widgets/mascot_slot_test.dart`.

**Storage, schema, and i18n.** None. The mascot stays decorative, so there are no new strings.

**UI.** Nothing moves. The mug is part of the mascot's own drawing.

**Rollback.** Revert the change. Nothing persisted changes shape.
