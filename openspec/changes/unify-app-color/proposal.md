## Why

Sara's phone runs `theme_palette = custom:351` (a background palette) with `accent_color = moss`
(the accent). The mascot takes its colors from the background palette; routine cards and buttons
take theirs from the accent. Two independent settings mean the mascot and the buttons don't match,
and she doesn't want that: "It's not necessary to have two colors, the theme color and apart the
accent colors. The accents are fine, I like those more. The theme colors are not needed anymore."

Decided with her: the background follows the accent. One choice colors everything.

## What Changes

- `AppTheme.light`/`dark` take a single `Palette` (default `Palette.inkIris`) instead of a
  background palette and a separate accent. That one palette's own scheme, neumorphic pair, and
  mascot become the whole theme — surfaces included. `Palette.withAccent`, the merge step that used
  to graft an accent's primary identity onto a different background scheme, is deleted along with
  its tests, since there's no longer a second scheme to merge into.
- `main.dart` watches only `accentSettingProvider`.
- The background palette setting is gone: `PaletteSetting`, `paletteSettingProvider`, and
  `AppPrefs.paletteId`/`setPaletteId` are deleted. The `theme_palette` value already on a device's
  disk is left untouched — nothing reads it any more, so there's nothing to migrate.
- `PalettePicker`, its swatch widget, and its custom-hue bottom sheet are deleted from
  `app/lib/widgets/`. Settings › Appearance now shows only the accent picker under the Appearance
  header. `accentName` moves into `accent_picker.dart`, the only file that still needs it.
- `Palette.defaultPalette`, `Palette.fromStorageId`, and the custom-palette storage plumbing
  (`customPrefix`, `customId`, `isCustom`, `storageId`) are deleted — nothing calls them once the
  background palette setting and its custom-hue sheet are gone. `Palette.fromSeed` and the built-in
  palette list are untouched: every built-in but warm paper is still generated by it.
- `settingsAccentHelper` becomes "Colors the whole app." / "Colorea toda la app.", since the accent
  no longer colors only routine cards and buttons. `settingsThemeTitle`, `settingsThemeHelper`, the
  six `theme*` palette-name strings, and the three `themeCustom*` strings are deleted from both ARB
  files — nothing renders them once the palette picker is gone.
- `DESIGN.md` is updated to describe one color choice instead of two, and to name `ink_iris` as the
  shipped default instead of `sea_glass`. Warm paper stays the normative reference palette every
  other palette is generated from — that doesn't change.

**This removes the background palette and its custom-hue sheet from Settings.** Someone setting up
a fresh install today sees a Sea Glass page with Ink & Iris routine cards; after this change, a
fresh install is Ink & Iris throughout, page and cards alike. **Existing users see their accent's
palette everywhere** — Sara's phone will theme itself entirely in moss, mascot included, the next
time it starts. Nothing under `schemas/` changes, and the local database is untouched: this is a
device-level presentation setting, stored in `shared_preferences`, same as before.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `appearance-defaults` (from `fix-appearance-defaults`) — a fresh install's default is no longer a
  separate background-palette concern; it's entirely the accent's default now. The requirement
  describing the background palette's fresh-install fallback is removed.
- `accent-color` (from `add-accent-color`) — the accent is no longer merged into a separate
  background palette. It's the only color setting, and it drives the whole theme: surfaces, every
  `ColorScheme` role, the neumorphic pair, and the mascot. The requirements that existed only to
  describe the merge (independence from the background palette, cross-palette contrast, and the
  `surfaceTint` carve-out) are removed, and a new requirement states plainly that the accent is the
  whole theme.

## Impact

**Code.** `app/lib/theme/palette.dart` (delete `withAccent`, `defaultPalette`, `fromStorageId`, and
the dead custom-storage plumbing), `app/lib/theme/theme.dart` (`AppTheme.light`/`dark` take one
`Palette`), `app/lib/main.dart` (watches only the accent), `app/lib/state/app_prefs_provider.dart`
(delete `PaletteSetting`), `app/lib/services/app_prefs.dart` (delete the palette pref key),
`app/lib/widgets/mascot_slot.dart` (fallback mascot is `Palette.inkIris.mascot`), delete
`app/lib/widgets/palette_picker.dart`, `app/lib/widgets/accent_picker.dart` (gains `accentName`),
`app/lib/screens/settings/settings_screen.dart` (drop `PalettePicker`), both ARB files, `DESIGN.md`.
Tests: new `app/test/theme/theme_test.dart`, updated `app/test/theme/routine_card_colors_test.dart`
and `app/test/theme/appearance_defaults_test.dart`, deleted `app/test/theme/accent_test.dart` and
`app/test/widgets/palette_picker_test.dart` and `app/test/state/palette_setting_test.dart`, updated
`app/test/widgets/accent_picker_test.dart`, new `app/test/screens/settings/settings_screen_test.dart`.

**Storage and schema.** None. `schemas/*.json` and the Drift database are untouched — this is a
`shared_preferences` presentation setting, same as before. The `theme_palette` key, if a device has
one, is simply never read again; no migration writes to it or clears it.

**Existing users.** Anyone who had chosen a background palette different from their accent now sees
the accent's palette everywhere, including the mascot. Anyone who never touched either setting
already saw ink iris as their accent and keeps seeing it, now applied to the whole app instead of
only routine cards and buttons.

**i18n.** `settingsAccentHelper` changes wording in both `app_en.arb` and `app_es.arb`.
`settingsThemeTitle`, `settingsThemeHelper`, `themeWarmPaper`, `themeInkIris`, `themeSeaGlass`,
`themePlum`, `themeSlate`, `themeMoss`, `themeCustom`, `themeCustomTitle`, and `themeCustomHue` are
deleted from both files. `settingsAccentTitle`, `settingsThemeCurrent`, and every `accentColor*` key
are unchanged.

**Rollback.** Reverting restores the two-setting model: a separate background palette, its picker
and custom-hue sheet, and the accent merged only into routine cards and buttons. Nothing persisted
changes shape in either direction, so there's nothing to unwind on disk.
