## Why

The mascot's fur reads as spiky and sharp next to the rest of the app, which is soft and rounded. Sara wanted it closer to a rounder reference: chunkier tufts with blunt tips, a squat body, and soft light across the fur. She also liked one piece of an earlier experiment, where the eye smiles by a lid rising from below, and wanted to keep it in place of the current flat squash.

## What Changes

- Re-trace the fur outline from `rive/mascot/build/softer-reference.png`, fitted around the unchanged eye and at the current height, so the pet takes the same space on every screen.
- Add a colour-free shading overlay on the fur: white light from the top left, a little shade toward the bottom. It works with any body colour.
- Add a faint ground shadow, drawn in the accent colour like everything else outside the silhouette.
- In celebrate, step done, jump, and bounce, a body-coloured lid with an ink line rises from below the eye so it smiles. The eye keeps its shape instead of squashing flat.
